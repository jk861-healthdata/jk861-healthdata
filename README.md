# About me
###
I have recently undertaken further study in MSc Health Data Science at the University of Exeter. This is building from my first-class BSc (Hons) degree in Mental Health Nursing with the University of East Anglia where I graduated in 2025.

I also work full-time as a Senior Community Mental Health Nurse for the NHS and support my wife and son who I live with in Norfolk.

I am passionate about mental health and improving health outcomes for people. Working for the NHS as a Mental Health Nurse is as rewarding as it is challenging. While I enjoy working hard to improve the health outcomes of individuals and my local community, I am inspired to influence health at a population level.

My hope is that by completing this MSc in Health Data Science, I will have opportunities to work alongside researchers and data specialists who are directly involved in influencing healthcare policy and government strategy, healthcare commissioning, and new treatment pathways for people experiencing mental illness.

With key figures such as the President of the Royal College of Psychiatrists, Dr. Lade Smith CBE, stating that the UK is in a "silent mental health pandemic", now is the time for radical thinking and bold ideas to change the course of our country for the better. But without evidence, without data, meaningful change will not happen until it is too late to improve the health of our communities.
